## What it does

Policy Change Tracker monitors regulators, legislatures, and standards bodies in your jurisdictions, then delivers plain-language impact summaries: what changed, who it affects, and what to do by when.

## Key features

- **Jurisdiction watchlists** — track only the regions and topics you operate in.
- **Impact summaries** — plain-language briefs, not legal soup.
- **Deadline extraction** — compliance dates pulled into your calendar.
- **Control mapping** — changes linked to affected internal policies.
- **Weekly digest** — everything relevant, narrated and prioritized.

## Who it's for

Compliance, legal, and ops teams in multi-jurisdiction businesses.
